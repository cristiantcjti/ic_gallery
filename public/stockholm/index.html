International Convention - Stockholm - 2026

Experience

Note: Photos after the text. If you appear in any photo and would like us to remove it, please get in touch.

Since we had the opportunity to participate in the arrangements for the international convention in Brazil in 2019 and experienced all the joy and love at that event, a desire became ours — to be international convention delegates. We didn’t know when we would have the opportunity, but we were certain that once that opportunity arose, we would definitely try to take advantage of it.

It was then in mid-September 2025 that the announcement was read about the series of international conventions in 2026. What a joy it was to learn that not only would Brazil’s branch again host one of the conventions, but that it had also been invited to several other international conventions in 2026. When we heard the announcement, we didn’t quite know how, but we were certain that we would try to fulfill the dream of living the experience of being international convention delegates and feeling all the love that only exists within Jehovah’s organization. So we prayed, made some plans, and decided to fill out the application, and with Jehovah’s goodness and mercy, we were approved.

Well, in the midst of all the preparations, everything was going well until… on the day of the special talk, one week before the memorial, just before we were leaving for the meeting, we received a phone call — Dani’s mam had been run over while she was preaching from house to house. That marked the beginning of one of the most difficult periods of our lives up to that point. There were 63 days of hospitalization, during which we had to deal with many challenging circumstances: the blood issue, infections, uncertainties. But throughout this process, we saw Jehovah’s care, guidance, and blessings in many ways, until, with 12 days remaining before our trip, Dani’s mom was discharged and was able to go home. Jehovah is wonderful and once again showed that He is with us — 1 Pe. 5:10.

Only at that moment did we feel more confident about the certainty of our trip. Here begins our newest adventure, and we hope to capture as much as possible in this album and share with you, friends and family, these very happy moments — we hope you enjoy it.
