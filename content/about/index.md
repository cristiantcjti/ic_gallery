Welcome to the **International Convention Gallery**

We are Dani 👩🏻 and Cris 🙎🏻‍♂️.

We grew up together in the same congregation. We've known each other since we were very young, in fact since we were children. I remember Dani when I was 12 and she was 11, but she doesn't remember me from that time 😒😅. She says she remembers me from the day of my baptism when I was 14 and she was 13.

After many years and many stories, we got married in 2013. Since the beginning of our marriage, Dani has been serving as a regular pioneer. In mid-2017 we decided to support the English language and we've been in the English East congregation in São Paulo ever since. In 2019 I became a pioneer and was appointed as an elder, and we've had the privilege of serving Jehovah together as pioneers ever since.❤️

Jehovah gave us the great privilege of attending the international convention in Stockholm, Sweden.

That's why we decided to create this site to share this very special moment with our friends.

If you've found our site, it's because we already know each other or we're new friends. We hope you enjoy the photos and that we can share this experience with all of you.

Feel free to send us messages and thank you for visiting our site.
